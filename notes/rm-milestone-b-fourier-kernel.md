# Milestone B as an inversion self-duality of a rank-eight Fourier kernel

> **Status.** This note rewrites the final extension problem on the Fourier side. After Milestone A, the transform of the rank-six Kodaira--Spencer divisor bundle is a rank-eight kernel bundle. Milestone B is equivalent to a generically invertible morphism from its inversion-dual to itself. This does not yet construct that morphism, but it turns the remaining determinant into a concrete Fourier-bundle self-duality problem.

## 1. A two-step resolution of `i_*E`

Let `L=O_X(Theta)` and `D=Theta` be smooth. For

\[
0\to O_D^{\oplus3}\to E\to\Omega_D^1\to0,
\]

the standard resolution of `i_*O_D` is

\[
0\to L^{-1}\to O_X\to i_*O_D\to0.
\]

The conormal sequence

\[
0\to K_D^{-1}\to O_D^{\oplus4}\to\Omega_D^1\to0
\]
combined with the resolutions of `i_*K_D^{-1}` and `i_*O_D` gives

\[
0\to L^{-2}
\to L^{-1\,\oplus5}
\to O_X^{\oplus4}
\to i_*\Omega_D^1
\to0.
\]

Applying the horseshoe lemma to the extension defining `E` yields a locally free resolution

\[
\boxed{
0\to L^{-2}
\overset{d_2}{\longrightarrow}
L^{-1\,\oplus8}
\overset{d_1}{\longrightarrow}
O_X^{\oplus7}
\to i_*E
\to0.
}
\]

Its K-class is

\[
[i_*E]=7[O_X]-8[L^{-1}]+[L^{-2}].
\]

## 2. Fourier transform away from the origin

On the dual ppav, `O_X` transforms to an object supported at the origin. Hence on

\[
U=\widehat X\setminus\{0\}
\]

the `O_X^{7}` term disappears.

The negative line bundles `L^{-1}` and `L^{-2}` are WIT in degree four. Write

\[
\mathcal F_2:=R^4\Phi(L^{-2}),
\qquad
\mathcal F_1:=R^4\Phi(L^{-1}).
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{rk}\mathcal F_2=16,
\qquad
\operatorname{rk}\mathcal F_1=1,
\]

and the transformed resolution becomes, with the homological shifts inherited from the length-two resolution,

\[
\mathcal F_2
\overset{\widehat d_2}{\longrightarrow}
\mathcal F_1^{\oplus8}.
\]

Fiberwise, the dual of this map is the eight-section map

\[
\mathbf C^8
\longrightarrow
H^0(X,L^2\otimes P^{-1})
\]
whose image is generated by the five fixed gauge/Koszul sections and the three Kodaira--Spencer variation sections.

Milestone A says that these eight sections are independent for general `P`. Equivalently, `widehat d_2` is generically surjective.

Define its rank-eight kernel

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K:=\ker(\widehat d_2).
}
\]

Then on the Milestone-A open set

\[
\boxed{
R^2\Phi(i_*E)=\mathcal K,
\qquad
R^3\Phi(i_*E)=0.
}
\]

In K-theory on `U`,

\[
\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal K)
=16e^{\widehat\Theta/2}-8e^{\widehat\Theta}.
\]

In particular

\[
\operatorname{rk}\mathcal K=8,
\qquad
c_1(\mathcal K)=0.
\]

## 3. The dual block

Put

\[
A_D=E^\vee\otimes K_D.
\]

Grothendieck--Serre duality for the divisor inclusion gives

\[
RHom_X(i_*E,O_X)
\simeq i_*A_D[-1].
\]

Combining this with Fourier duality and fiberwise Serre duality gives, on the same generic open set,

\[
\boxed{
R^1\Phi(i_*A_D)
\simeq(-1_{\widehat X})^*\mathcal K^\vee.
}
\]

Fiberwise this is simply

\[
H^1(D,A_D\otimes P)
\cong
H^2(D,E\otimes P^{-1})^\vee.
\]

Both sides have rank eight after Milestone A.

## 4. Fourier form of the final extension

An extension

\[
0\to E\to W_D\to A_D\to0
\]
represents a class

\[
\varepsilon\in Ext^1(A_D,E).
\]

Fourier--Mukai full faithfulness sends it, on the Milestone-A open set, to a morphism

\[
\boxed{
\varphi_\varepsilon:
(-1)^*\mathcal K^\vee
\longrightarrow
\mathcal K.
}
\]

At a point `P`, this is exactly the middle connecting map

\[
H^1(A_D\otimes P)
\longrightarrow
H^2(E\otimes P).
\]

Therefore

\[
R\Gamma(D,W_D\otimes P)=0
\]
for general `P` if and only if `varphi_epsilon` is generically invertible.

Thus Milestone B is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{
\exists\,\varepsilon\in Ext^1(A_D,E)
\text{ such that }
(-1)^*\mathcal K^\vee\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal K
\text{ generically.}
}
\]

In other words, the final problem is an **inversion self-duality of the rank-eight Fourier kernel**.

## 5. Why this is useful

This formulation explains several earlier numerical features at once:

1. the middle matrix is `8 x 8` because `K` has rank eight;
2. Serre duality is built into the inversion-dual source;
3. the extension-space calculation in `rm-milestone-b-extension-space-collapse.md` says that the available self-dualities form a linear system of dimension at most ten;
4. the determinant of the connecting map is simply the determinant of `varphi_epsilon`.

It also points to two structural mechanisms which can now be tested directly:

- **stability/simple-kernel route:** if `K` and `(-1)^*K^vee` are stable of the same slope, any nonzero morphism between them is an isomorphism;
- **theta-group duality route:** the rank-sixteen semihomogeneous bundle `F_2` is the Fourier transform of `L^{-2}` and comes with the standard theta-group duality. One can ask whether the eight-dimensional subspace selected by the five gauge sections plus three Kodaira--Spencer sections can be chosen so that the induced kernel `K` is inversion self-dual.

The second mechanism is closely related to the nondegenerate theta-group bilinear forms used in Wirtinger-type multiplication formulas.

## 6. Next falsifiable targets

1. determine whether `K` is slope-stable for a generic admissible three-plane of Kodaira--Spencer classes;
2. construct one nonzero class in `Ext^1(A_D,E)` after the extension-space collapse;
3. if stability holds, conclude Milestone B immediately from 1+2;
4. independently, express `widehat d_2` in the level-two theta basis and test whether theta-group duality identifies `(-1)^*K^vee` with `K`.

Either route would settle the middle determinant without computing an arbitrary `8 x 8` determinant entry by entry.
